How visits are counted

A visit is counted if you're away at that time of day. Change these if your routine is different.

  • Away at bedtime: overnight, $80 (dinner, sleepover, and next morning's breakfast).
  • Away at midday: midday visit, $40.
  • Away at breakfast but home the night before (like leaving at 5am): breakfast visit, $40.
  • Away at dinner but home by bedtime: dinner visit, $40.
  • 20% tip is always added.
